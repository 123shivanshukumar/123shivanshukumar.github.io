# A page for musicians 
Well music is an interest I did not discuss with a lot of people openly mostly because my taste differs from most of those around me significantly -- I am  benevolent (check usage) fan of Indian Classical Music -- why is not clear to me -- somehow it came naturally but Asara Near has some insights on how our taste of music might be related to our perceptions 

Having said that I want to elab on the concept of a RAGA.


In my opinion therefore a RAGA is more like a description of a particular mood -- as opposed to the common notion of treating them like a set of notes. 
And therefore there is some or the other RAGA that is inside my head most of the times -- this significantly impacts the way I think and there is some sort of an environment created around me -- this is one of those things I find the hardest to explain
